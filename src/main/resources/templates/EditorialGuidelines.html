Manuscript & Editorial Guidelines

Complete standards for author submissions, typography formatting, copyright compliance, and peer appraisal on READORA.

2026 Revised Standards Active

Important Submission Protocol: All manuscripts submitted to READORA Studio undergo preliminary intake appraisal by the Senior Literary Curator followed by peer evaluation. Ensure all citations and translated excerpts comply with the copyright guidelines below before submitting.

1

Manuscript Requirements

READORA accepts original monographs, anthologies, academic dissertations, and literary collections. Every submission must represent a complete work rather than fragmented chapter drafts.

  • Minimum Length: Works must exceed 60 standard pages (15,000 words) for full volumes, or 25 pages for poetry collections.
  • Supported Formats: Uploads must be compiled in clean PDF/A standard or validated EPUB 3.0.
  • Structure: Include a title page, frontispiece/epigraph (optional), structured table of contents, and numbered chapters.
2

Formatting Guidelines

To preserve aesthetic legibility in the READORA Reader interface, your digital file must follow baseline typographic consistency.

Typography & Body:

Standard serif fonts (Georgia, Playfair, Garamond) at 11–12pt body text with 1.4–1.6 line height.

Margins & Layout:

Minimum 1-inch (2.54 cm) page borders with mirrored gutters for dual-page viewing modes.

3

Language & Quality Standards

Submissions in English, standard Nepali (Devanagari script), and authorized bilingual formats are accepted.

  • Nepali Texts: Must use UTF-8 standard Unicode Devanagari fonts (Preeti or legacy non-Unicode fonts are strictly rejected).
  • Proofreading: Works must undergo thorough spelling and syntax proofing prior to stage-two editorial review.
4

Copyright & Originality

Authors retain 100% intellectual property rights. Submitting your work to READORA grants non-exclusive digital distribution licensing under Nepal Copyright Act 2059 and South Asian Berne Convention protocols.

Plagiarism detection scans are automatically executed across all uploaded PDFs. Works with uncredited third-party content will be flagged and rejected immediately.
5

Cover Image & Metadata Standards

Cover Artwork Specifications:
  • Dimensions: 1600 x 2400 px (2:3 aspect ratio)
  • Color Profile: RGB / sRGB, min 300 DPI
  • File Format: High-quality JPG or PNG (< 5MB)
  • Title & Author Name must match metadata exactly
Mandatory Metadata:
  • Exact book title and secondary subtitle
  • 150–250 word curated synopsis / abstract
  • Category selection (e.g., Himalayan Memoirs, Philosophy)
  • Valid ISBN-10/13 or READORA Digital ID assignment
